# 04 Crisis playbook and escalation matrix
Owner: Brand Communications (simulated) | Date: 2026-10-01

- **S1** approver: Head of Communications; SLA 15 min. Suggest only: case brief, evidence and holding-statement option; Head of Communications decides and acts.
- **S2** approver: PR Manager; SLA 60 min. Draft from approved template; PR Manager sign-off before anything is published.
- **S3** approver: Social Media Manager / Customer Care Lead; SLA 240 min. Template reply with one-step approval by Social Media Manager / Customer Care Lead.
- **S4** approver: none (template / weekly audit); SLA 1440 min. Pre-approved low-risk template may be sent and logged; weekly sample audit.
